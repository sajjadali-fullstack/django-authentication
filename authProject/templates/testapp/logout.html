Sajjad Exams - General Instructions

Important Note: Agla exam shuru karne se pehle niche diye gaye rules ko dhayan se padhein.
Rule-1: Time Management

Once you click the start button, the timer will begin. Each exam has a specific time limit, and it will auto-submit when the time expires.

Rule-2: No Refresh / Back

Do NOT refresh the page or press the back button during the test. Doing so might clear your progress or auto-submit your incomplete response.

Rule-3: Final Submission

Make sure to review all your answers before hitting the final Submit Exam button. Once submitted, you cannot modify your responses.

Rule-4: Active Session

You must remain logged in throughout the session. If your session expires or you clear your browser cookies, you will be locked out of the exam.
