Instructions & Rules

Please read all the instructions carefully before starting the exam.
  • Rule-1
    Time Management: Once you click the start button, the timer will begin. Each exam has a specific time limit, and it will auto-submit when the time expires.
  • Rule-2
    Navigation & Refresh: Do NOT refresh the page or press the back button during the test. Doing so might clear your progress or auto-submit your incomplete response.
  • Rule-3
    Submission: Make sure to review all your answers before hitting the final Submit Exam button. Once submitted, you cannot modify your answers.
  • Rule-4
    Authentication Requirement: You must remain logged in throughout the session. If your login session expires or you clear your browser cookies, you will be locked out of the exam.
